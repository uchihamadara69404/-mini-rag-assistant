Other topics similar to AI, machine learning, and embeddings include neural networks, deep learning, natural language processing, and vector databases for semantic search.
## Core related topics
- **Deep learning** – Multi-layer neural networks used for tasks like image recognition, speech, and language understanding.
- Neural networks – Computational models inspired by the brain that learn patterns from data, forming the basis of many modern ML systems.
- Natural language processing (NLP) – Techniques that let computers understand and generate human language, often powered by text embeddings.
## Topics around embeddings
- Vector databases – Specialized databases that store embedding vectors and power semantic search, recommendations, and similarity queries.
- Dimensionality reduction (PCA, UMAP) – Methods that compress high-dimensional data into lower-dimensional embeddings while preserving structure.
- Graph embeddings – Techniques like Node2Vec that represent nodes and edges as vectors for tasks like link prediction and fraud detection.
## Modern AI system building blocks
- Large language models (LLMs) – Models that use embeddings at multiple levels (tokens, sentences, documents) to perform generation and reasoning.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) – Systems that combine semantic search over embeddings with generation to answer questions using external documents.
- Recommendation systems – Models that use user and item embeddings to suggest relevant products, content, or connections.
## Practical ML topics
- Classic ML algorithms – Logistic regression, SVMs, decision trees, and k-means used together with embeddings as features for prediction and clustering.
- Model evaluation and monitoring – Techniques for tracking performance, drift, and embedding-space anomalies over time in production systems.
- Data quality and labeling – Using embeddings to detect outliers, reduce noise, and improve training datasets for downstream AI and ML models.
